2) How the Structural Tags Are Used: 

- header: The page header contains the course name, page title, and navigation. Used for introductory content or navigation links. 
- nav: Groups the links to other pages in the site. Indicates navigation. 
- main: identifies the page's primary content and contains the articles, headers, and sections
- article: Groups the complete instructional content about structuring pages.Has multiple sections inside it. There is a second header here which introduces the article.
- section: divides the article into multiple sections such as "Sectioning elements," “Document outlines,” and “WAI-ARIA Roles.” Contains aside.
- aside: Contains the “W3C Warning.” It is inside a section because warning relates to that topic. 
- footer: Contains the CSUN link and address. 

==========================================================================

3) ARIA Roles:

- banner: identifies the page header.
- navigation: identifies the navigation part of page, the links. 
- main: identifies the primary content.
- complementary: identifies the aside. 
- contentinfo: identifies the footer.
